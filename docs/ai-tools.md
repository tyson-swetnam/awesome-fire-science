# :material-robot: AI & Productivity Tools

AI assistants and productivity tools can enhance research workflows, automate repetitive tasks, and accelerate data analysis. This page covers general-purpose AI tools, productivity software, and specialized geospatial AI models relevant to wildland fire science.

??? Tip "Important: Privacy & Security"
    Always check your agency or institution's IT policy before using AI tools with work data. Do not upload sensitive, proprietary, or confidential information to public AI services. Verify all AI-generated outputs for accuracy and do not rely on them without validation.

## :material-earth: AI for Geospatial & Remote Sensing

### :material-satellite-variant: Foundation Models for Earth Observation

[:material-satellite-variant: MOSAIKS](https://www.mosaiks.org/){target=_blank} - is a system that allows users to quickly transform vast amounts of imagery into maps of new variables, using their own training data.

[:material-satellite-variant: Prithvi](https://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial){target=_blank} - IBM-NASA geospatial foundation model trained on NASA satellite imagery for various Earth science tasks.

[:material-earth: Clay](https://clay-foundation.github.io/model){target=_blank} - Open-source foundation model for Earth observation with temporal and multi-modal capabilities.

[:material-vector-polygon: Segment Anything (SAM)](https://aidemos.meta.com/segment-anything/){target=_blank} - Meta's foundation model for image segmentation, applicable to satellite imagery analysis.

[:material-vector-polygon: SAM-Adapter](https://github.com/tianrun-chen/SAM-Adapter-PyTorch){target=_blank} - Adaptation of Segment Anything Model specifically for remote sensing applications.

### :material-map: AI-Powered GIS Tools
