# :material-brain: Training

This comprehensive directory organizes wildland fire training resources by audience and career stage. From entry-level firefighter certifications to specialized smoke management courses, from tribal cultural burning programs to university doctoral research, these resources serve the full spectrum of fire science professionals and land managers.

## 1. :material-school-outline: Getting Started in Wildland Fire

For students, career changers, and entry-level firefighters beginning their journey in wildland fire.

??? Tip "NWCG Foundation Courses"

    [National Wildfire Coordination Group Courses](https://www.nwcg.gov/training/training-catalog){target=_blank} provide the foundation for wildland fire qualifications nationwide. Core courses include S-130 (Firefighter Training), S-190 (Introduction to Wildland Fire Behavior), and position-specific training for engine operators, crew bosses, and incident management teams.

??? Tip "Youth & Early Career Programs"

    [Student Conservation Association Wildland Fire Academy](https://thesca.org/sca-wildland-fire-academy){target=_blank} - 6-month paid positions ($650-$1,000/week) with full NWCG certification on Shasta-Trinity National Forest. Hands-on training preparing participants for federal fire careers.

    [California Conservation Corps](https://ccc.ca.gov/training/firefighting/){target=_blank} - Operates 26+ hand crews at 13 locations with monthly stipends potentially exceeding $32,000 first year. Entry-level wildland firefighting with year-round crew assignments.

    [Veterans Fire Corps](https://www.veteransfirecorps.org/){target=_blank} (Conservation Legacy/SCA/AmeriCorps) - 12-week to 6-month assignments with 94% of participants reporting the program prepared them for fire employment. Targeted toward veterans transitioning to civilian fire careers.

    [Clackamas Community College](https://www.clackamas.edu/academics/departments-programs/wildland-fire-management-aas){target=_blank} (Oregon) - Four certificate pathways certified by PNWCG and NWCG, instructors with 15-30 years experience, includes EMT certification.

    [Lane Community College](https://www.lanecc.edu/programs-academics/areas-study/science-natural-resources-math-and-engineering/wildland-fire-management){target=_blank} (Oregon) - Includes traditional cultural burning practices from Pacific Northwest Indigenous peoples alongside standard NWCG training.

    [Lake Superior College](https://www.lsc.edu/degrees/wildland-firefighting-certificate/){target=_blank} - One-year Wildland Firefighting Certificate preparing students for seasonal fire positions.

    [Minnesota North College](https://minnesotanorth.edu/programs/wildland-firefighting/){target=_blank} (formerly Itasca Community College) - Eight NWCG courses with partnerships across federal and state agencies, one of the strongest community college programs in the Midwest.

??? Tip "Federal Agency Entry Pathways"

    [USFS Wildland Fire Apprenticeship Program](https://www.fs.usda.gov/managing-land/fire/careers){target=_blank} - Department of Labor-accredited apprenticeships providing structured career pathways in the Forest Service.

    [USFWS Wildland Fire Apprenticeship](https://www.fws.gov/program/fire-management/get-involved){target=_blank} - Launched 2022, DOL-accredited program with 12-48 month pathways including details to helitack, hand crews, rappel crews, and fire use modules. First class included 28 apprentices.

    [BIA Pathways Internship Program](https://www.bia.gov/service/pathways){target=_blank} - Connects Native American and Alaska Native college students to paid fire management internships across BIA and tribal programs.

## 2. :octicons-organization-24: Federal Agency Training

For federal firefighters and resource specialists working across agencies.

??? Tip "Multi-Agency Coordination"

    [NAFRI Courses](https://www.nafri.gov/courses){target=_blank} - National Advanced Fire and Resource Institute provides advanced training for incident management teams and overhead positions.

    [National Interagency Prescribed Fire Training Center](https://talltimbers.org/fire-training/){target=_blank} - Established 2021 at Tall Timbers Research Station, providing multi-agency prescribed fire training with emphasis on southeastern fire ecology.

??? Tip "USFS Programs"

    [United States Forest Service Training](https://www.fs.usda.gov/managing-land/fire/training){target=_blank} - Comprehensive fire training programs for Forest Service personnel.

    [Interagency Fire Program Management Standard (IFPM)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/managing-land/ifpm){target=_blank} - Standardized fire program management across federal agencies.

    [Fire Ecology Curriculum (Southwestern Region)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/educational/educators/fire-ecology-learning-lab){target=_blank} - Ecological foundations for fire management in southwestern ecosystems.

    [Smokejumper Rookie Training](https://www.fs.usda.gov/science-technology/fire/people/smokejumpers){target=_blank} - 5-week intensive program at bases in Missoula, McCall, Redding, and seven other locations. Requires 3-4 years fire experience (hotshot preferred), with specific fitness standards: 7 pull-ups, 45 sit-ups, 25 push-ups, 1.5-mile run under 11 minutes, and 110-lb pack test.

??? Tip "BLM Training"

    [BLM National Training Center](https://www.nifc.gov/about-us/our-partners/blm/training){target=_blank} - Phoenix-based facility offering distance learning through DOI Talent platform for BLM fire personnel nationwide.

    [Great Basin Training Unit](https://www.blm.gov/programs/fire/regional-info/nevada){target=_blank} - Delivers approximately 60 courses annually to over 1,000 personnel across the Great Basin region.

    [BLM Alaska Fire Service](https://fire.ak.blm.gov/){target=_blank} - Maintains the "Handy Dandy" firefighting field guide and coordinates Alaska-specific training addressing unique northern fire conditions.

    [BLM Idaho Boise Helitack](https://www.nifc.gov/about-us/our-partners/blm/blm-helitack-crews){target=_blank} - Operates the only DOI Type 1 helicopter (UH-60 Blackhawk), providing specialized aviation training opportunities.

??? Tip "National Park Service"

    [Wildland Fire Resource Advisor Course (N-9042)](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1965/wildfire-training.htm){target=_blank} - Online course certifying 500-700+ students annually in providing resource protection advice during incidents.

    [Non-Fire Employee Surge Capacity Training](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1965/wildfire-training.htm){target=_blank} - Trains rangers, interpreters, and other NPS staff to support fire operations during peak seasons.

??? Tip "US Fish & Wildlife Service"

    [USFWS Wildland Fire Apprenticeship](https://www.fws.gov/program/fire-management/get-involved){target=_blank} - DOL-accredited program launched 2022, with details to helitack, hand crews, and rappel operations. Note: USFWS applies 50% of all DOI prescribed fire acres.

??? Tip "Bureau of Indian Affairs"

    [Wildland Fire First Aid Program](https://www.nifc.gov/about-us/our-partners/bia/training){target=_blank} - Medical Incident Technician and Medical Incident Leadership training levels. Over 740 students from 50+ Indian Country fire crews have completed certified classes, representing approximately 60% of Indian Country's permanent fire workforce.

## 5. :material-certificate-outline: Professional Certifications

For practicing professionals seeking credentials and certifications.

??? Tip "State Certified Prescribed Burn Manager Programs"

    **Alabama** - 32-hour course, $50 fee, 5-year certification.

    [California (CA-RX) Burn Boss](https://www.fire.ca.gov/programs/resource-management/resource-protection-improvement/prescribed-fire/){target=_blank} - Burn Boss certification with 40-hour classroom training plus task book. 55 active certified as of December 2025.

    [Florida Certified Prescribed Burn Manager](https://www.fdacs.gov/Forest-Wildfire/Wildland-Fire/Florida-Prescribed-Burn){target=_blank} - 24-hour course ($175), approximately 1,800 certified practitioners statewide.

    [Georgia Certified Prescribed Burn Manager](https://gatrees.org/fire-prevention-suppression/prescribed-burn-certification/){target=_blank} - Written exam requiring 70% or higher, 2 years experience, 5 prescribed burns led.

    [Mississippi Certified Prescribed Burn Manager](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/prescribed-burning){target=_blank} - 1-day or 3-day options with 80% pass requirement.

    [Missouri Certified Burner](https://mdc.mo.gov/your-property/fire-management/prescribed-fire){target=_blank} - Online course ($25) through Missouri Department of Conservation, leads to Certified Burner status.

    [Oklahoma Prescribed Fire](https://extension.okstate.edu/programs/prescribed-fire/){target=_blank} - Oklahoma State University Extension self-paced course, 9 modules covering prescribed fire fundamentals.

    [Oregon Certified Burn Manager](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/fire-program/certified-burn-manager){target=_blank} - Two certification levels (pile burning; pile and broadcast burning), provides civil liability protection under state law.

    **Pennsylvania, South Carolina, Tennessee, Virginia, Texas** - Various state-specific requirements and durations.

    [Texas A&M AgriLife Extension Prescribed Burn Manager](https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/){target=_blank} - Certified and Insured Prescribed Burn Manager preparation program.

??? Tip "NWCG Specialized Qualifications"

    [Fire Behavior Analyst (FBAN)](https://www.nwcg.gov/positions/fire-behavior-analyst){target=_blank} - Requires S-490, S-491, and completed task book. Provides fire behavior analysis and forecasting on incidents.

    [Long Term Fire Analyst (LTAN)](https://www.nwcg.gov/positions/long-term-fire-analyst){target=_blank} - Requires satisfactory FBAN performance plus S-590. Provides extended fire behavior forecasting for complex incidents.

    [NRCS Technical Service Provider](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/conservation-by-state/technical-service-providers){target=_blank} - Prescribed Burning (Code 338) certification enables recommending burns as a conservation planning alternative for agricultural producers.

??? Tip "Fire Investigation Certifications"

    [IAAI-CFI](https://www.firearson.com/Certifications){target=_blank} (International Association of Arson Investigators Certified Fire Investigator) - Requires 5+ years experience, 400 hours documented training, Pro Board and FSAB accredited.

    [NAFI-CFEI](https://www.nafi.org/certification){target=_blank} (National Association of Fire Investigators Certified Fire and Explosion Investigator) - Oldest and most widely held fire investigation certification, based solely on NFPA 921.

    [CCAI-CFI](https://www.arson.org/){target=_blank} (California Conference of Arson Investigators Certified Fire Investigator) - Three levels (Technician, CFI, Master), requires 75 fires for origin determination and 75 for cause investigations.

??? Tip "Wildland-Urban Interface Certifications"

    [NFPA Certified Wildfire Mitigation Specialist](https://www.nfpa.org/education-and-research/certifications/wildfire-mitigation){target=_blank} - Demonstrates professional competence in wildfire hazard mitigation and WUI protection.

    [IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home](https://wildfireprepared.org/){target=_blank} - Free online learning with three designation levels (Base, Plus, Plus with Ventilation) available in California, Oregon, Nevada, and New Mexico.

    [International Code Council IWUIC Training](https://www.iccsafe.org/products-and-services/i-codes/wildland-urban-interface-wui/){target=_blank} - International Wildland-Urban Interface Code training, adopted statewide in California, Utah, Montana, Nevada, Pennsylvania, and Washington.
