Connect with wildland fire science communities, discussion forums, professional networks, and collaborative platforms.

## :material-forum: Discussion Forums

[:material-fire: Wildland Fire Lessons Learned Center](https://wildfirelessons.net){target=_blank} - Forum for sharing experiences, lessons learned, and best practices in wildland fire management.

[:simple-reddit: r/Wildfire](https://www.reddit.com/r/Wildfire){target=_blank} - Reddit community for wildland firefighters, managers, and researchers. Active discussions on current incidents, careers, and policy.

[:simple-reddit: r/GIS](https://www.reddit.com/r/gis){target=_blank} - GIS professionals community with frequent fire-related geospatial discussions and technical support.

[:simple-reddit: r/RemoteSensing](https://www.reddit.com/r/RemoteSensing){target=_blank} - Satellite imagery analysis, Earth observation, and remote sensing science discussions.

[:simple-reddit: r/FireEcology](https://www.reddit.com/r/FireEcology){target=_blank} - Academic and scientific discussions on fire ecology, prescribed burning, and fire management.

[:material-stack-overflow: GIS StackExchange](https://gis.stackexchange.com){target=_blank} - Q&A site for GIS professionals with excellent technical support for geospatial analysis.

## :material-hand-heart: Contributing to Open Science

Many fire science tools and datasets are open source and welcome contributions:

- Report bugs and request features via GitHub Issues
- Contribute code, documentation, or tutorials
- Share your datasets and workflows
- Peer review open manuscripts on platforms like PREreview
- Participate in hackathons and code sprints
- Answer questions on forums and StackExchange
- Organize or present at local user groups and meetups

See [Getting Started](getting-started.md) for pathways to participate in collaborative fire science!
