Africa¶

Regional Subsahara Africa Wildland Fire Network (Afrifirenet) - coordinated through Global Fire Monitoring Center

Southern Africa Fire Network (SAFNet) - fosters collaborative efforts in fire monitoring and management in southern Africa through remote sensing and geospatial technology (SAFNET Overview PDF)

SADC Regional Forest Fire Management - Southern African Development Community guidelines and capacity building for forest fire management

South African National Space Agency (SANSA) Fire Monitoring - uses Earth observation satellite imagery for fire monitoring and management

Africa Fire Mission - supporting fire management practices in African savanna-protected areas

Near East & North Africa¶

Near East and North Africa Fire Network (NENFIRE) - FAO network covering 20 countries addressing forest fire threats across Algeria, Bahrain, Egypt, Iran, Iraq, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Libya, Mauritania, Morocco, Oman, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Sudan, Syria, Tunisia, UAE, and Yemen

European Forest Fire Information System (EFFIS) - European Commission Joint Research Centre fire monitoring system

FIRE-RES - Integrated fire management strategy development across 11 European living labs including Spain, Portugal, France, Greece, Italy, Bulgaria, Nordic countries, and Central Europe, focusing on extreme wildfire events

European Forest Institute

Sub-Regional Euro-Alpine Wildland Fire Network - Alpine region fire management collaboration

Regional Mediterranean Wildland Fire Network - Mediterranean basin fire management network

Silva Mediterranea Forest Fire Working Group - FAO working group for Mediterranean forest fires

ForestWISE (Portugal) - Portuguese collaborative laboratory for integrated forest & fire management

Master Fuego (Spain) - Spanish fire management training and education program
