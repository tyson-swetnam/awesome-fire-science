AI & Productivity Tools¶

AI assistants and productivity tools can enhance research workflows, automate repetitive tasks, and accelerate data analysis. This page covers general-purpose AI tools, productivity software, and specialized geospatial AI models relevant to wildland fire science.

Important: Privacy & Security

Always check your agency or institution's IT policy before using AI tools with work data. Do not upload sensitive, proprietary, or confidential information to public AI services. Verify all AI-generated outputs for accuracy and do not rely on them without validation.

Generative AI Assistants¶

General-purpose large language models (LLMs) that can assist with code generation, data analysis, writing, and research tasks.

ChatGPT - OpenAI's conversational AI with advanced reasoning capabilities, code generation, and creative writing. 🆓 Free tier available, 💰 $20/mo Plus with GPT-4 and extended capabilities.

Claude - Anthropic's AI assistant with 200k token context window, excellent for long document analysis, research synthesis, and technical writing.

Google Gemini - Google's multimodal AI with deep integration into Google Workspace, supporting text, image, and data analysis.

Microsoft Copilot - Microsoft's AI assistant integrated into Windows, Edge browser, and Office 365 applications.

Perplexity - AI-powered search engine that provides sourced answers with citations, useful for research and fact-checking.

Fire Science-Specific AI Applications¶

As AI tools continue to evolve, watch for specialized applications in wildland fire science. Current examples include:

Allen AI Wildlands - AI research initiative focused on wildfire prediction, detection, and management using machine learning.

WIFIRE Firemap - Near real-time fire mapping using AI and satellite imagery.

Productivity & Automation Tools¶

Microsoft Office 365 & Copilot¶

Microsoft 365 - Enterprise productivity suite with AI capabilities.

  • Word Copilot - AI writing assistance, document drafting, and editing suggestions
  • Excel Copilot - Data analysis, formula generation, and chart creation
  • PowerPoint Copilot - Slide design, content generation, and presentation creation
  • Outlook Copilot - Email drafting, scheduling, and inbox management

Google Workspace & Gemini¶

Google Workspace - Cloud-based productivity suite with integrated AI.

  • Docs - Collaborative writing with AI assistance
  • Sheets - Spreadsheet analysis with Gemini integration
  • Slides - Presentation creation with AI-powered design
  • Gmail - Smart compose and email organization

Automation & Scripting¶

Claude Desktop - Desktop app with extended context, file analysis, and MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration for connecting to local tools and data sources. 🆓 Free tier available.

GitHub Copilot - AI pair programmer that suggests code completions and entire functions. 💰 $10/mo individual, free for students and educators.

Cursor - AI-first code editor built on VS Code with intelligent code generation and chat interface.

Zapier - Workflow automation connecting 5,000+ apps, from simple triggers to complex multi-step workflows.

Power Automate - Microsoft's automation platform for creating workflows across Office 365 and other services.

IFTTT - "If This Then That" automation for connecting web services, smart home devices, and apps.

Note-Taking & Knowledge Management¶

Notion AI - All-in-one workspace with AI writing assistant, database management, and project tracking.

Obsidian - Local-first markdown note-taking with powerful linking, plugins, and AI integrations.

NotebookLM - Google's AI research assistant that can analyze uploaded documents and generate insights.

Mem - AI-powered note-taking that automatically organizes and surfaces relevant information.

Voice & Transcription¶

Otter.ai - Real-time meeting transcription with speaker identification and searchable notes. 🆓 Free tier with 300 monthly minutes.

Whisper - OpenAI's open-source speech recognition model with high accuracy across languages.

Descript - Audio/video editing with automatic transcription, overdub voice synthesis, and collaborative editing.

AI for Geospatial & Remote Sensing¶

Foundation Models for Earth Observation¶

MOSAIKS - is a system that allows users to quickly transform vast amounts of imagery into maps of new variables, using their own training data.

Prithvi - IBM-NASA geospatial foundation model trained on NASA satellite imagery for various Earth science tasks.

Clay - Open-source foundation model for Earth observation with temporal and multi-modal capabilities.

Segment Anything (SAM) - Meta's foundation model for image segmentation, applicable to satellite imagery analysis.

SAM-Adapter - Adaptation of Segment Anything Model specifically for remote sensing applications.

AI-Powered GIS Tools¶

Esri GeoAI - ArcGIS integrated AI/ML tools for spatial analysis, image classification, and predictive modeling.

Picterra - Cloud-based AI platform for geospatial object detection with custom model training.

Azavea Raster Vision - Open-source framework for deep learning on satellite and aerial imagery.

TorchGeo - PyTorch library for geospatial data with pre-trained models and datasets for remote sensing.

Wildfire-Specific ML Models¶

WIFIRE Firemap - Near real-time wildfire detection and mapping using satellite imagery and machine learning.

FireCast - Pyregence's ML-powered fire behavior forecasting system for California.

Stanford Next Day Wildfire Spread - Deep learning model for predicting next-day wildfire spread using weather and terrain data.

HPWREN SmokeyNet - Wildfire smoke detection using HPWREN camera network and convolutional neural networks.

Additional Resources¶

AI for Earth - Microsoft's program providing cloud computing resources and AI tools for environmental research.

Google Earth Engine - Cloud-based planetary-scale geospatial analysis with integrated ML capabilities (see also Computing).

Kaggle - Datasets, competitions, and notebooks related to wildfire prediction and analysis.
