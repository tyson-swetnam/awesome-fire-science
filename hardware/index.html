Hardware & Equipment¶

Physical tools and equipment for wildland fire research, monitoring, and field operations.

Portable Power¶

Jackery - Portable power stations from 240Wh to 3kWh capacity for charging laptops, drones, and equipment in the field. 💰 $200-$4,000 depending on capacity.

Goal Zero - Solar generators and portable power banks with integrated solar panels. 💰 $100-$3,500.

EcoFlow - Fast-charging portable power stations with high output capacity for power-hungry devices. 💰 $200-$4,000.

Anker - Compact USB power banks and portable chargers for phones, tablets, and small electronics. 💰 $20-$200.

Titan Solar Generator - High-capacity off-grid power systems with lithium battery banks. 💰 $3,000-$10,000+.

Satellite Communication¶

Starlink - SpaceX satellite internet with portable and mobile options for high-bandwidth field connectivity. 💰 $599 hardware + $150/mo service.

Iridium GO! - Satellite hotspot enabling voice calls, texts, and limited data anywhere on Earth. 💰 $800-$1,200 + monthly service.

Garmin inReach - Two-way satellite communicators with SOS, messaging, and tracking. 💰 $300-$500 + monthly service.

Zoleo - Satellite messaging device for SMS and email when cellular coverage unavailable. 💰 $200 + $20-$50/mo.

SPOT - Satellite GPS messenger with SOS and tracking features. 💰 $150-$250 + annual service.

Weather Stations¶

Tempest Weather System - Wireless all-in-one weather station with haptic rain sensor, no moving parts, solar powered. 💰 $329 .

Davis Vantage Pro2 - Professional-grade weather station with wind, temperature, humidity, rainfall, and optional sensors. 💰 $700-$1,500.

Ambient Weather - Consumer weather stations with WiFi connectivity and online dashboards. 💰 $150-$600.

Kestrel Weather Meters - Handheld weather meters for wind, temperature, humidity, and heat stress measurements. 💰 $150-$800.

HOBO Data Loggers - Temperature, humidity, and environmental data loggers for long-term monitoring. 💰 $100-$500.

Drones & UAVs¶

DJI Mavic 3 - Compact drone with 4/3 CMOS camera, 46-min flight time, and obstacle avoidance. 💰 $2,000-$5,000 depending on configuration.

DJI Matrice 30T - Enterprise drone with thermal imaging, zoom camera, and laser rangefinder for fire operations. 💰 $10,000-$14,000.

Autel EVO II - 8K camera drone with 40-min flight time and thermal imaging options. 💰 $1,500-$7,000.

senseFly eBee X - Fixed-wing mapping drone for large-area surveys with up to 90-min flight time. 💰 $12,000-$25,000+.

Skydio 2+ - Autonomous flight drone with advanced obstacle avoidance using AI. 💰 $1,100-$2,200.

Drone Regulations

US operators must follow FAA Part 107 rules for commercial use. Check local regulations, airspace restrictions, and fire incident TFRs (Temporary Flight Restrictions) before flying. Coordinate with incident commanders when operating near active fires.

Cameras & Sensors¶

FLIR Scout TK - Handheld thermal imaging camera for spotting heat signatures and hotspots. 💰 $600-$1,000.

Reconyx - Professional trail cameras for wildlife and time-lapse monitoring of vegetation and fire effects. 💰 $400-$700.

Bushnell Trophy Cam - Budget-friendly trail cameras for field monitoring. 💰 $100-$300.

GoPro - Action cameras for documenting field operations and research, mountable on helmets, vehicles, or drones. 💰 $200-$500.

Insta360 - 360-degree cameras for immersive documentation of fire behavior and operations. 💰 $300-$800.

Measurement Tools¶

Laser Rangefinders - Laser distance meters and rangefinders for measuring tree height, plot boundaries, and distance to fires. 💰 $100-$1,000.

Tree Calipers & DBH Tape - Traditional forest measurement tools for diameter at breast height (DBH) and tree coring. 💰 $20-$200.

LiDAR Sensors - Laser scanning systems for detailed 3D mapping of terrain and vegetation structure. 💰 $10,000-$100,000+ depending on specifications.

Suunto Clinometers - Handheld instruments for measuring slope, height, and angles in the field. 💰 $200-$400.

Ruggedized Computers¶

Panasonic Toughbook - Military-grade rugged laptops and tablets for harsh field conditions. 💰 $2,000-$6,000.

Dell Latitude Rugged - Semi-rugged and fully-rugged laptops for field computing. 💰 $2,000-$5,000.

Getac Tablets - Rugged Windows tablets with sunlight-readable displays and long battery life. 💰 $1,500-$4,000.

Samsung Galaxy Tab Active - Rugged Android tablets with replaceable batteries and S Pen support. 💰 $400-$600.

Communication Hardware¶

Garmin GPSMAP - Handheld GPS units with topographic maps and satellite communication options. 💰 $300-$700.

Two-Way Radios - VHF/UHF radios for team communication in the field. Check frequency regulations. 💰 $100-$1,000+ per unit.

Mobile Hotspots - Cellular hotspots and routers for internet connectivity via multiple carriers. 💰 $200-$1,500.

Navigation & Safety¶

Suunto Compasses - Precision compasses for navigation and mapping. 💰 $30-$200.

Paper Maps & Waterproof Cases - Waterproof notepads, field books, and map cases for analog documentation. 💰 $5-$50.

Personal Locator Beacons (PLBs) - Emergency beacons for SOS signaling via satellite. 💰 $250-$500, no subscription required.

Safety Equipment¶

Always follow agency safety protocols and carry required personal protective equipment (PPE) including fire shelter, helmet, fire-resistant clothing, gloves, and eye protection when working near wildland fires.

Portable Fire Extinguishers - Vehicle-mounted fire extinguishers for fire line safety. 💰 $50-$300.

Safety Glasses & Goggles - Eye protection from smoke, ash, and debris. 💰 $10-$100.

Respirators & Dust Masks - N95/N100 respirators for smoke and particulate protection. 💰 $1-$50 per unit.
